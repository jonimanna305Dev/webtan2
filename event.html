Event FAQs

Answers to common questions about our event ideas and future activities.

How can I get information about an event?
You can contact WebTen through our contact page to ask about event plans, availability, and participation details.
Are the events online or in person?
The event format will depend on the activity and its requirements. Confirmed event details should specify whether participation is online, in person, or hybrid.
Who can participate in WebTen events?
Participation requirements may vary. Depending on the event, activities may be suitable for students, developers, entrepreneurs, or technology professionals.
Do I need to register before attending?
Registration requirements will be announced when an event is confirmed. Please check the event details or contact WebTen for more information.
